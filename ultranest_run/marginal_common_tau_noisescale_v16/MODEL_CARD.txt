Fully Bayesian run: the integrand is the MARGINAL likelihood
(galaxy template integrated out in closed form under a proper
Gaussian prior), so the samples are a posterior and logz is a
Bayesian evidence under the following priors:
  * template g ~ N(0, lam^-1 I_P), P = 576,
    lam = 6.42800309e+00  (sigma_g = 3.944228e-01 cts/s/px);
    empirical-Bayes scale = lam_rel_init * max diag H at the
    nominal registration, tau' = 0 and the NOMINAL weights
    (f = 1), computed once and frozen;
  * per-epoch offsets: uniform on +/- 1.5 px about
    the nominal registration (epoch-0 gauge);
  * per-epoch noise scales f_i, i = 0..7 (v07):
    ln f_i uniform on [ln 0.25, ln 10], i.e. log-uniform
    f in [0.25, 10]; the weights enter as w_i / f_i^2 and the
    Gaussian normalization carries -n_i ln f_i, n_i = 215, 215, 215, 215, 215, 215, 215, 215;
  * tau': flat on [0, 1.0] (the evidence scales with the
    inverse of this ceiling).
theta layout: offsets[0:14], lnf[14:22], tau[22]  (tau' last; N = 8 epochs,
ndim = 23).
lnL includes the theta-independent constants c_data
(7.87707703e+03) and P/2 ln lam; evidence
DIFFERENCES between variants on the same weights are unaffected
by c_data, but comparisons across different lam require the
P/2 ln lam term (included).
Reported values are re-evaluated with reference_logl (float64).
Robustness (sensitivity.json, computed from THIS run by
importance reweighting -- no extra sampling): the template-
prior ladder lam x {1/10, 1/3, 3, 10}, and the v05 soft-log
tau' prior
p(tau') = 1/[(tau' + 0.01) ln(1 + 100)];
each row lists tau' quantiles, Kish ESS (validity certificate)
and dlnZ.  An ESS collapse (< ~5% of the points) is the signal
that a variant needs its own run; not expected here.
Audit numbers (verify()) are in verify.json next to this file.
